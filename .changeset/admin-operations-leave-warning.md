Leaving Operations asks first, Exit returns, global queues name their forms, and erasers get a known-target screen.

- A link out of Operations opens a Stay or Leave dialog that names where it goes and says
  the selected environment does not apply there. Stay is focused first, Escape is Stay, and
  movement inside Operations, its global queues and the personal routes asks nothing.
- Exit Operations returns to the last address outside Operations the tab visited, and falls
  back to the other area's landing when there is none.
- The dead-letter queue and the erasure log name each row's form when the caller may read
  it, and the dead-letter page drops its whole-installation form picker.
- `/operations/forms/{formId}/erase` is a form's Erase screen beside Intake, shown only to a
  holder of that form's erase cell: it asks for a session id, says whether that session
  exists in the selected environment, and erases through the response detail's
  confirmation. The assistant's screen tables carry it.
