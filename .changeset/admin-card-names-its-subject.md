A card names the form or question it acts on, not its id.

- A step whose subject the plan does not carry is named from the forms list and the question
  library the operator can already open, through a read that reaches no further than those
  screens do.
- The id behind a resolved title stays in the collapsed Identifiers block.
- A plan acting on several questions at once falls back to the tail of each id, so its steps
  never read as the same sentence; one step on its own keeps the plain word.
