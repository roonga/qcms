Give the authoring app its new visual foundations (issue #1121): Inter for UI text and
JetBrains Mono for data, the SigPilot navy dark ramp, no card shadows, 4 / 6 / 2 corners
with 8px for what floats, a 15px base with 600 titles, one border weight, fewer boxes, a
tonal secondary button, and a 4px spacing scale.

The Code Owner ruled on 2026-10-24 that the app read as brutalist, amended that ruling the
same day to adopt the foundations of its sister product, and then added the spacing scale
and the density rules. `plan/soften-proposal.md` carries the measurements, the amendments
and what was actually built.

`@roonga/qcms-ui` is in this release because the font registry is: `inter-400.woff2` and
`jetbrainsmono-400.woff2` are replaced by the variable `inter-variable.woff2` and
`jetbrainsmono-variable.woff2`, so a 600 title is a real instance rather than a
synthesized one, and the portal's font switcher serves its 400 from the same file. The
committed font payload goes from 710,984 to 754,812 bytes across the same 24 files. No
portal palette, face or floor changes: the respondent surface is a separate redesign.

The token sheet is still generated by `plan/admin-theme/build.mjs`, whose WCAG gate now
also holds the two secondary states, the hover and inset surfaces, and the bounding edge
on all four grounds. Every pair passes in Light, Dark and High contrast; High contrast is
otherwise untouched and keeps both its 2px borders and its solid secondary.
