The assistant's cards are written for a form builder.

- A step names the form or question it acts on by title, taken from the plan's own draft or
  from the draft open in the builder, and falls back to the address it was asked for.
- An id a title stands in for moves behind a closed Identifiers disclosure.
- Card wording drops builder vocabulary: not published yet rather than unpublished draft,
  version 1 rather than draft v1, address rather than slug, uses rather than pins.
- A proposed question is named by its label in the draft diff and in the pending-question
  note.
- A plan card is headed by what the plan is for and how many steps it takes.
- A counted title takes its plural form, so one link reads as one test link.
- A card says the name of the language a form is authored in.
