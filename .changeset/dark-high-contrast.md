Add the dark high-contrast token layer: `.dark.hc` on the scope carrier, plus a dark accent for harbor, sand and plum. High contrast is now polarity crossed with contrast, and a theme owes a `.dark.hc` accent only when its light-HC accent misses 7:1 against the dark field. The kit also re-exports the switch and radio-group primitives an app's own chrome needs.
