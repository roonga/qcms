The boundary to design around

Model decides

  • Which tools to call, and in what order
  • How to read the customer's intent
  • What to say in the reply
  • Whether to propose a refund or escalate

Harness enforces

  • Refund limits and the 30-day window
  • Human approval above a threshold
  • Idempotency: no double refunds
  • Step, cost and time budgets
  • PII redaction and the audit log

What you can say afterward

I built an agent harness for a customer-support refund workflow. It enforces policy outside the model, gates risky actions behind human approval, makes writes idempotent, redacts PII, keeps an audit log, and is tested with an evaluation suite that includes prompt-injection scenarios.
