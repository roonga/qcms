# A date in the future

**Use for:** a travel date, an appointment, a start date, a date cover is wanted from.

**Settings**

- `date`, `required` as the form needs it.
- `constraints.min`: the date you are authoring on, written out in full.
- `constraints.max`: a ceiling the form can defend, such as one year on from the date you are authoring.
- `messages.min` in the form's own words: "This date cannot be in the past."

**Cannot be expressed**

- "Not in the past", as a rule that stays right. The lower limit is a fixed date.
- "Within the next 90 days", or any window measured from the day the form is filled in.
- A limit read from another answer.

**Say this**

> The earliest date it accepts is today's date, as a fixed date rather than a moving one. From tomorrow it will accept yesterday, so this one needs looking at from time to time.

**Corpus sources:** ahm travel insurance claim (cancellation and additional expense claims), NSW driver licence application, Australian Medicare enrolment MS004.
